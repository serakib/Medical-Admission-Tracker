1. Privacy Policy

Medical Admission Tracker (“the website”, “we”, “our”) provides study practice, account profiles, progress tracking, leaderboards, friends features, notifications and virtual reward points. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use those features.

Information that may be processed

  • Account and identity: Firebase user identifier, sign-in email where provided by the authentication provider, account type, display name and profile image/avatar.
  • Profile details: information you choose to add, such as college and batch.
  • Learning activity: practice/exam attempts, answers and scores, completion counts, accuracy, XP, rank points (RP), PB balances, levels, streaks and related progress summaries.
  • Social and preference data: friend requests/connections, profile visibility choices, notification preferences and other settings you use.
  • Safety and administration records: account status, guest-account deletion requests and schedules, approval decisions, and limited audit records needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Technical data: information automatically supplied by your browser, hosting provider or security services, such as request details and diagnostic/error information. The exact logs depend on those providers and their settings.

How information is used

  • To authenticate accounts and keep the correct account signed in.
  • To save and display your profile, study progress, balances and leaderboard position.
  • To operate friend, notification, reward, guest-account and administrator features.
  • To detect errors, prevent abuse, protect accounts and investigate service issues.
  • To respond to support, correction or deletion requests.

Public and private information

Some profile information and leaderboard statistics may be visible to other visitors or signed-in users, depending on the feature and visibility settings. Do not put private contact information or sensitive personal details in a public display name, avatar, college/batch field or other public-facing profile field.

Email addresses, authentication credentials, guest tokens and private account-management information are not intended to appear on public profiles or public leaderboards. Administrative access is restricted by role and should be used only for legitimate service-management and safety purposes. No administrator should ask you to share your password or sign-in token.

Service providers and where data is processed

The current website code integrates Firebase Authentication and Firebase client libraries. The application also communicates with a Google Apps Script backend that reads and writes application records in Google Sheets. The site loads third-party resources such as fonts, icons and front-end libraries from external providers. Those providers may receive technical request information when your browser connects to them and process data under their own terms and privacy notices.

We do not claim that all information stays on your device or in one country. Processing and storage locations depend on the configuration and infrastructure of the relevant providers. Review Google/Firebase and hosting-provider privacy information for their independent processing practices.

Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described above, shown through features you choose to use (such as public profile or leaderboard features), or disclosed where required to respond to valid legal requests or protect users and the service. We do not promise that internet transmission or third-party infrastructure is risk-free.

Security

We use account authentication, server-side role checks and access restrictions in the application. You should use a unique password, keep your device secure and sign out on shared devices. No website can guarantee absolute security. If you believe an account or private information has been exposed, contact us promptly.

Retention and deletion

Information is kept for as long as needed to operate the account and its features, meet legitimate operational or security needs, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable requirements. Guest deletion may involve a scheduled waiting period and an approval workflow shown in the website. A timeout or network error does not prove that a request succeeded or failed; check the account status before submitting a duplicate request. Some backups, provider logs or legally required records may persist for a limited period after an account deletion workflow completes.

Your choices

  • Use the website's profile and visibility settings to control information that those settings expose.
  • Ask to access or correct account/profile information, or request account deletion, through the available account controls or the contact details below.
  • You can clear browser storage through your browser settings, but doing so may sign you out or remove locally cached preferences; it does not delete server-side account records.

Children and student users

This is an educational service that may be used by students. If you are under the age at which you can independently consent to online data processing under the laws that apply to you, use the service only with the involvement and permission of a parent or legal guardian where required. Parents or guardians who have a privacy concern may contact the site owner. Do not submit sensitive personal information.

Changes

This notice may be updated when features, providers or legal requirements change. The updated version and effective date will be posted on this page.

2. Cookie & Browser Storage Policy

The website may use browser storage technologies. Not all browser storage is technically a cookie; this section covers both cookies and similar local storage.

Essential storage

  • Authentication/session: Firebase and the browser may use storage or cookies to maintain sign-in and security state.
  • Guest account continuity: a guest identifier or guest token may be stored in browser local storage so a guest can return to the same account. Treat the guest token like a password and never share it.
  • Preferences and cached data: the website may store profile snapshots, leaderboard snapshots, selected settings or interface preferences to improve loading and preserve your choices.

Third-party resources

Firebase, Google-hosted fonts and other external libraries may make requests to their own domains. Those providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies according to their settings and policies. This website does not claim to control third-party storage.

Managing storage

You can block or clear cookies and local storage in your browser. Blocking essential storage may prevent sign-in, guest account recovery, saved preferences or some features from working. Clearing local storage does not automatically erase information held by the application backend or its providers.

We do not describe non-essential advertising or analytics cookies as active unless those services are actually configured. If optional tracking or analytics are added later, this policy and any required consent controls should be updated before they are enabled.
